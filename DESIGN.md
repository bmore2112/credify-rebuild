# Credify Rebuild — Design System (ui-ux-pro-max)

**Direction:** "Editorial Fintech" — premium, confident, magazine-grade. Differentiates from the generic fintech-blue category (the BEST sites are clean but samey). Dark ink canvas + warm ivory + a single gold accent, with a serif/grotesk pairing that supports the brand's signature *italic emphasis* headlines ("Making the _impossible_ possible").

## Tokens
- **Ink** `#0B0B0F` (bg) · **Ink-2** `#141419` (cards) · **Line** `#26262E`
- **Ivory** `#F5F2EA` (primary text) · **Muted** `#A0A0AB`
- **Gold** `#D4A24E` (accent / CTAs) · **Gold-hi** `#E7BD6A`
- **Funded green** `#34D399` (success / positive proof only)
- **Radius** 14px cards / 999px pills · **Shadow** soft, low-opacity
- **Type:** Display = **Fraunces** (variable, real italics for emphasis); UI/Body = **Inter**
- **Scale:** clamp-based fluid type; H1 clamp(2.6rem, 6vw, 5rem)

## Principles (from RESEARCH best-vs-worst)
- ONE primary CTA ("Get Your Custom Quote"), repeated — never competing buttons, no pop-ups.
- Specificity = trust: real stats everywhere ($100K / $840M / 99% / 100+).
- Transparent 4-step process; honest, accessible multi-step form with "no credit pull".
- Strong trust band (clients, disclosures, identity) for YMYL/E-E-A-T.
- A11y: semantic landmarks, visible labels, focus rings, prefers-reduced-motion, AA contrast.
- Perf: static-first, system-font fallback, lazy images, CWV budget.
